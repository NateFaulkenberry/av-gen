# ADR-898: Band-limited onsets -- kick, snare, hat

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-09-26
**Found by:** the GV3 revision audit (reports/modulation.md §6.7): onsets were broadband only, so the
first pass of Glowmere Valley 3 hand-scored 475 kick times into a timeline source.
**Follows:** ADR-004 (the analyzer's broadband onset), ADR-870 (the replayed bus)
**Implemented by:** `detectBandOnsets`, `stampBandOnsets` (`src/analysis/band_onsets.hpp/.cpp`),
called from `AnalysisTrack::analyze`; the batch merge in `Engine::consumeAnalysis`; the live overlay
`overlayTrackFields` (`src/analysis/analysis_track.cpp`); `audio.onsetLow/Mid/High`
(`src/signals/audio_signals.cpp`)
**Tools:** `avgen_music_report AUDIO --kicks FILE` scores the low onsets against a kick list
**Tests:** `tests/unit/test_band_onsets.cpp`, `tests/integration/test_audio_features_bus.cpp`,
`tests/integration/test_rebuild_analysis.cpp` (`[onsets]`, skips without the song)

## Context

`audio.onset` fires on anything that starts: a kick, a clap, a hat, a bass note. A route that wanted
the kick could not say so. On "Rebuild" the obvious detector -- low-band flux -- is useless: the
bass is sidechain-pumped and retriggered on the off-beats, so 20-150 Hz flux fires about as often
between kicks as on them (precision 0.2, recall 0.5 against the measured kicks). Even in the band
where the kick's pitch sweep is strongest, 100-300 Hz, a bass pluck on the "and" of 4 looks like a
kick in level, rise and decay.

## Decision

Three onset streams, detected over the whole track after beat tracking (so a detector may look a few
frames ahead), stamped on the analysis frames and published as events.

**low -- the kick.**

1. *Harmonic/percussive separation* (Fitzgerald 2010) on the bins below ~500 Hz: a 17-frame median
   across time keeps the horizontal lines (a held or gliding bass), a 17-bin median across frequency
   keeps the vertical ones (a drum), and a soft Wiener mask splits each bin between them.
2. *Candidates*: peaks of the percussive power in 100-300 Hz that reach 3x its running median
   (+-0.5 s) and are the largest within 90 ms. The onset is stamped on the steepest one-frame rise
   before the peak -- the attack, not the crest.
3. *Classification*, by three measures: the peak's size against the band's largest within +-1 s
   and against the track's own scale (at least 10% -- -10 dB -- of the 90th percentile of its candidate
   attacks, so a snare's low body is not "the strongest thing nearby" through a bar with no kick), and
   the percussive share of 40-300 Hz over the attack and the four frames after it (a drum is
   percussive there; a plucked note mostly harmonic). **On the tracked beat grid** (within 0.12 of a
   beat) little evidence is needed (5% and 0.05); **off it**, as much as the strongest kick nearby
   (50% and 0.5). "Rebuild"'s kick is 3-4x any off-beat hit (01-music.md §1.4), and in most music the
   kick is what defines the beat -- but a strong off-beat kick still passes. With no grid: 20% and
   0.12.

**mid -- snare/clap**: peaks of 2-6 kHz power (3x the running median, 30% of the band's local
largest, 50 ms apart). **high -- hats**: 6-16 kHz (10%, 40 ms). No separation: in those bands the
percussion is the dominant transient.

Each onset's strength is its peak against the band's largest within +-1 s, 0..1; the bus floors a
fired event's value at 0.05 so a quiet hit still moves an event route.

**On the bus**: `audio.onsetLow`, `audio.onsetMid`, `audio.onsetHigh`, declared with the frame signals
so a seek replays them. `Engine::consumeAnalysis` now ORs every event across the analysis frames a
render frame consumes -- the band onsets and the tracked beat, as it always did the broadband onset --
so none is lost at a low frame rate.

**Offline only, by construction.** Live input has no band onsets (the three events never fire, and
the onset rate counts the broadband onset). Live *playback of a loaded file* overlays the whole
track's band onsets and beats on the runner's frames over each frame interval (`overlayTrackFields`),
so the editor fires the kicks a render fires.

**Where an artist finds them:** in the route-source picker as `audio.onsetLow - kick (low-band
onset)`, `audio.onsetMid - snare / clap (mid-band onset)` and `audio.onsetHigh - hats (high-band
onset)`; `signal.list` finds "kick", "snare" and "hats".

## Consequences

- **"Rebuild"**, against the 475 kicks of `tools/gv3/music.py kicks()` within +-30 ms (re-measured
  2026-09-26 with the list generated by that function): 480 low onsets, 465 matched -- **precision
  0.969, recall 0.979**, mean error +1.7 ms, worst 26.7 ms. None in the drum-less pull-back (bars
  15-16); the half-strength muffled kick of the break (bars 89-92) is found. Of the 10 misses, 6 are
  kicks of the riser's roll (bars 93-96) that the detector stamps 31-45 ms late, so each also counts
  among the 15 extras; four more extras sit on beat 4 of bars 24, 40, 56 and 72, the kick gaps,
  where the bass turnaround hits, three on the "and" of beat 4 (bars 17, 19, 33) and one 15 ms before
  the kickless downbeat of bar 15. The other misses are the downbeats of bars 13, 51, 59 and 67.
- Without the grid prior the same detector reaches precision 0.86 / recall 0.92: the residual false
  kicks are percussive bass plucks on the off-beat after beats 2 and 4.
- **Synthetic groove** with a percussive bass note on every off-beat: precision and recall >= 0.95,
  and fewer than 5% of the off-beats fire; the broadband onset fires on more than half of them.
- mid and high onsets land on the backbeat claps and the off-beat hats (recall >= 0.9 on the
  synthetic groove). On "Rebuild" the mid band also catches the kick's click and the high band the
  clap -- band-limited, not a drum classifier. By position on the grid: of 562 mid onsets, 229 sit on
  beats 2 and 4 (the claps), 85 on beats 1 and 3, 136 on off-beats and 112 off the 8th grid; of 842
  high onsets, 436 are the off-beat hats, 230 sit on beats 2 and 4 and 96 on 1 and 3; of 480 low
  onsets, 470 sit on beats.
- **Changed behaviour:** `audio.beat` and the band onsets reach every render frame's publish (before:
  a beat on any but a batch's last analysis frame was dropped, about one in three at 30 fps). The
  broadband `audio.onset` is unchanged.
  - The Glowmere Valley 2 saucer (`visitor`) has a `spin` behaviour on `audio.beat`, so on the
    multicam film it now turns on every beat, and its path through the film differs.
  - That exposed a latent ADR-891 defect in the continuous take: its last shot's aim follow ended
    with no join, so the film's final frame (226.30 s, one frame past the fold's 226.29 s) dropped
    the follow offset at once -- a 71.7-degree cut, caught by ADR-892's no-snap benchmark. The take's
    last follow entry now lets its offset go over a join (`installSequence` in
    `src/app/camera_director.cpp`, continuous mode only), checked in
    `tests/unit/test_continuous_shot_multicam.cpp`.
- **Cost:** the separation reads ~13 bins x 34 medians per frame -- tens of milliseconds for a
  four-minute track, once, at load.
- **GV3's hand-scored `timeline.kick`** can be replaced by `audio.onsetLow`, with one difference:
  its keys lead the kicks by 20 ms (GV3's `LEAD_SECONDS`), while the detector's onsets sit 1.7 ms
  after them on average and fire on the first render frame at or after that.
