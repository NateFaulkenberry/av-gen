# Crowd Navigation Lab

The lab for the navigated crowd (115 programme; ADR-2220, ADR-2223, ADR-2225, ADR-2229). It asks one question:
**where did the crowd walk, why there, and did anyone walk through anyone?**

## Environments

Generated by `examples/labs/crowd/build.py` (stdlib Python). Edit the script, not the JSON. A CPU test runs
`build.py --check` and fails if the committed files differ from its output byte for byte.

| File | Environment | What it isolates |
|---|---|---|
| `e01-flat-plaza` | Two groups of 25 cross between four areas, three times | avoidance between groups; arrival in an area; the gait on a walking body |
| `e02-obstacles` | A wall with a 3 m and a 2 m opening; a field of 2 m blocks | routing round authored walls; two routes to one place |
| `e03-bottleneck` | A 24 x 20 m room emptied through a 2.0 m gap; 200 agents (tests take 50, 100, 200) | jams, oscillation and penetration in a bottleneck; the step cost |
| `e08-replay` | Two wandering groups with staggered departures, then a goto and a hold | seek = play; thread independence |

Environments 4-7 (intersecting groups, multiple levels, dynamic obstacles, 500-agent performance) arrive with
Stage B phase B3, and with the navigation mesh for multiple levels.

Everything is procedural or CC0: a flat procedural terrain, the crowd block's walls, and the tracked CC0 alien
(`assets/imported/alien.gltf`, `assets/imported/ATTRIBUTION.md`) at scale 0.02, whose walk is 1.06 m/s and run
2.29 m/s (measured off its toes).

## Cases

`examples/labs/crowd/cases.json`. Case numbers are 100 x environment + k. Each case's expectation is asserted in
`tests/unit/test_crowd_world.cpp`:

- **101:** the flat plaza is crossed with no overlap above 0.03 m.
- **104:** a walking character's planted toe does not skate (median slip at most 15% of body speed).
- **201:** obstacles are passed with every route found.
- **301–303:** the bottleneck at 50, 100 and 200 agents.
- **801:** a checkpointed seek equals a from-zero replay at ten random seconds.
- **802:** the result is the same on 1, 4 and 8 threads.
- **803:** a play equals a cold seek.

## Reading the debug layer

Use `--debug-draw crowd`, or the lab's overlay profile.

Agents:
- Each agent is a disc of its radius with a facing tick.
- The disc's colour is its state:
  - grey: idle;
  - yellow: departing (waiting out its stagger);
  - cyan: moving;
  - green: arrived;
  - violet: dwelling at a wander point;
  - red: unreachable;
  - orange: wedged, meaning slower than 0.05 m/s for over 2 s while moving.

Paths and walls:
- A moving agent's remaining corridor is a blue polyline to its goal.
- The goal is a white cross.
- Authored walls are white segments.

A red disc, or an orange disc that stays orange, is a navigation failure. `CrowdWorld::state().pathStatus` names the
reason: noStart, noGoal, unreachable or exhausted.

## Determinism

The crowd at second t is the state after `floor(t x 60)` fixed steps (ADR-2225):
- the steps are Jacobi;
- neighbours are taken in a fixed order;
- path queries are counted per step, never timed;
- every random choice is a hash.

A seek restores the nearest checkpoint (one per second of film) and replays from it. The drawn pose between grid
instants is extrapolated and never fed back.

Destination areas must hold the group at a spacing a body can walk between (about 1.4 m for 0.3 m agents); a packed area wedges the last arrivals, who are then counted arrived within 2.4 m of their slot (ADR-2229).
