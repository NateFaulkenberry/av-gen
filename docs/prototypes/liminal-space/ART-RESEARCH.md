# Liminal Euclidean World: art research

*The art agent, 2026-09-30. This covers §14's visual, audiovisual, colour and camera parts, re-weighted by the
owner's addendum (`01-addendum-emotion.md`): "What does it feel like to be lost inside this world?". The
procedural-graphics part is the engineer's (`ENGINEERING.md`). The song is analysed in `SONG-ANALYSIS.md`, and
the research is turned into shots in `DIRECTOR-PLAN.md`.*

*Method: two web research passes, one on architecture, liminality and camera, the other on audiovisual mapping and
colour, synthesised and judged here. Source tags: **[P]** the primary source was fetched and read; **[S]** a
secondary source was read; **[SR]** seen in search results only, so the claim rests on the snippet; **[M]** from
memory, unverified. Every URL was fetched or appeared in search results. Treat [SR] and [M] claims as leads, not
facts.*

## The ten rules the research gives this video

Each rule is adopted because it serves the thesis (a lost person wandering an impossible, beautiful, empty building
who finds a way forward). The evidence is in the sections below.

1. **The camera is the lost person, not a drone.** It moves at human height and walking pace, it hesitates and
   takes indirect routes, and it never cuts away from travel. *Russian Ark, Gerry/Elephant, Tarr, Stalker.*
2. **Give the wandering a beacon.** One warm light, glimpsed again and again through doorways and stairwells, tells
   a viewer with the sound muted that there is somewhere to reach. *Journey, Dear Esther.*
3. **Repeat with one small change.** The same corridor, landing or doorway comes back with a single difference,
   anchored by a landmark so the viewer can compare. That is how "Have I been here before?" is produced without
   horror. *The Exit 8, P.T., Hammershøi, Freud's "involuntary repetition".*
4. **The doorway is the unit of rhythm, and the phrase is the unit of time.** Thresholds are passed on phrase
   downbeats. Visible synch points are punctuation at phrase ends, never a pulse. *Star Guitar, Hammershøi's
   enfilades, Chion's synch points, Gondry's "stopping just before it's too much".*
5. **Light is the emotional subject and the gentlest impossibility.** Sun falls where no sun can reach, a day sky
   hangs over lamplit rooms, and a plain door opens onto a bright void. *Hopper, Magritte, Turrell, Kentucky Route
   Zero.*
6. **A tiny figure seen from behind in a vast plain volume** gives identification and scale. When it disappears,
   the camera takes over its gaze. *Friedrich's Rückenfigur, Hammershøi, Journey, Monument Valley.* It is optional
   and never approached, never turned to face us.
7. **Change continuously, and hide only what should not be noticed.** Proportions, light and palette morph
   continuously. Structural swaps happen only behind an occluder (a dark threshold, a corner). An impossibility that
   is meant to be felt must be shown in one view, because changes made out of view go unnoticed (1 in 77 in Suma's
   study). *Escher's Metamorphosis, Suma et al., P.T., 1917.*
8. **Colour is feeling, added and withheld.** Start near grey. Add one hue family per chapter. Withhold the release
   hue everywhere until the release, and let it be seen first through a doorway. Give tension a tight single-hue
   gamut. Use two timescales: a slow voice with about seven states over the song, and a small fast voice inside
   each. *GRIS, WALL-E, The Wizard of Oz, Gurney's gamut masks, Scriabin's two-voice luce part.*
9. **Map sound through emotion, keep only congruent couplings, and let silence be real.** Bass is large, heavy,
   slow and low. Highs are small, fine and bright. Roughness is local and jagged. Loudening reads as acceleration,
   quietening as descent. A breakdown works only because what came before it moved. *Palmer 2013, Whiteford 2018,
   Evans and Treisman 2010, Eitan and Granot 2006, Solberg 2014, Chion.*
10. **The exit is clarity, not spectacle.** A side door onto fog and water, walls dissolving into even light, a
    let-go. *Russian Ark's ending, Turrell, Journey ("a relief. It's a let-go"), Superliminal.*

## 5. From the existing POC: what carries over and what changes

The Procedural Space POC's art notes (the previous art agent, 2026-09-30) found what made its look work. The owner
asked to keep its stylised look but leave the infinite hall. Adopted and changed:

| POC finding | here |
|---|---|
| Depth from **aerial perspective**: fog and background the same colour, surfaces dissolving into one air. | **Kept.** It is what makes rooms feel too large. The air becomes pale and luminous (dawn haze, grey light) instead of deep violet, because the addendum forbids "oppressive darkness for its own sake". |
| **The light is in the air**: a volumetric march, lamps with halos receding with the architecture. | **Kept** as the beacon's light and the window shafts. Halos are few; one warm source per space. |
| **Luminous edges, fogged** (ADR-1004): the edge term fading with distance. | **Kept, retuned**: a faint pencil-like line on creases (Escher's lithographs are line drawings), neutral or warm, never cyan neon. |
| **Restraint of an accent colour** (green only in the farthest lamps). | **Kept as the withheld hue** (section 4). |
| Nearly black, rough material. | **Changed** to pale matte plaster whose colour the palette drives. |
| No film grain; lower volumetric jitter; bloom 0.25 at threshold 1. | **Kept.** |
| **The music owns the rules, not the geometry**; routes with seconds-long attack and decay; "the music's authority grows" macro. | **Kept, reshaped by the addendum**: the authority does not grow monotonically. It follows the contrast arc (quiet, curiosity, energy, intimacy, silence, growth, tension, release) and is lowest in the breakdown and loose again at the end. |
| Kaleidoscopes, recursion, symmetry operators, scene-state switching. | **Dropped.** They demonstrate the renderer; they are the "states" the owner rejected. |

## 6. Deliberately not adopted

- **Horror mechanisms** from P.T., The Shining, the Backrooms and The Exit 8: threat, chase, jump reveals, sickly
  fluorescence, black-dominant frames. Their structures (the loop, the corner, the anomaly, the baseline) are kept
  and their reveals are made beautiful.
- **Orthographic illusions** (Monument Valley, OK Go) as the main device: they need one fixed viewpoint, and our
  camera walks. We build real topology instead, and allow at most one alignment moment.
- **Spectacle folds** (the Inception city fold, kaleidoscopes, the POC's rose window): they fail the addendum's final
  test ("does this make the viewer feel something, or merely demonstrate something the renderer can do?").
- **Rainbow and cyclic palettes, beat-synced flashing, strobe sync** (Ikeda): the brief's §20 list.
- **Print Gallery / Droste zooms and Powers of Ten**: striking, but tonight they would be demonstration. Candidates
  for LIVE or a later pass.
