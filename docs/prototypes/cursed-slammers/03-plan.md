## 2. Rendering approach (from 01 §3)

1. **Depth planes around a key plane.** Real geometry at exaggerated depths (01 §4), the camera aimed at the face.
2. **Specular lens ridges.** A `normal` output from `worldProject` + `palette` (vertical lenticules, 50-70 per unit),
   tilt ≤ 0.2 on skin and 0.6-0.8 on the substrate, plus anisotropic roughness along the ridge and `roughnessFilter`.
3. **The interlaced flip, the scene's core transition.**
   `t = smoothstep(lo, hi, saturate(G * viewDir.x + stripe(x) + bias))`, with `stripe = s * cos(2π x · pitch)`.
   - **Look A ("embossed")** is the face drawn as substrate: near-black, ridged, glossy. It is visible only in glints.
   - **Look B** is the lit ape: skin colour times the scan texture, wet speculars, eyes.
   - `bias` is routed (`material/cs_skin/op/<n>/constant` w) from the cycle. G (view gain) sets how strongly the
     camera's sweep moves the curtain.
   - A **third look** (the silver negative with a faint violet glow) is the Disturbance layer, reached by a second
     mix on its own bias.
4. **Muted foil metal** on the bars and substrate (palette F0 around silver).
5. **The dissolve effect** on the hands (`reverse`, noise, a silver edge colour, edge emission ≤ 3) for the
   selective in-and-out, with the flip on the face. The two mechanisms are deliberately different, so the layers
   come and go *differently*.
6. **Crosstalk accent:** in Disturbance, the camera position gets 3-4 stepped micro-jumps (a `step` track added to
   the arc) with the echo at 3 frames, 0.4.

## 3. The apparition cycle (one 64 s loop, existing tools only)

- **Mechanism:** one `timeline` source per envelope (`timeline.ether`, `timeline.contact`, `timeline.hands`,
  `timeline.face`, `timeline.eyes`, `timeline.disturb`, `timeline.recede`). Each has `loopLength` 64. Each is pure
  in time, so seeking and scrubbing land exactly. Routes map them onto parameters.
- **Reset by construction.** Every key at 64.0 equals its key at 0.0, so the cycle cannot accumulate state, pop or
  stick.
- **The camera** loops at 32 s (two sweeps a cycle) and is never reset.
- **Seconds, not beats**, for robustness in quiet passages and without a confident beat. Musical alignment is an
  option (D4).

| Phase | Time (s) | What happens | Driven by |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Ether | 0-8 | Near dark. The substrate's ridge combs catch the travelling sweep light. The embossed face (Look A) is a barely perceptible relief in the glints. Bars almost unlit | `timeline.ether` holds `sweep` intensity low; the flip bias at "A" |
| 2 First contact | 8-14 | One bar catches a thin, sharp highlight (the sweep peaks). Then a knuckle's wet highlight (the hand's dissolve reveals two fingers). Then a sliver of the brow as the flip curtain first crosses its ridge | `timeline.contact`: sweep peak; the hand dissolve to 0.8; the flip bias edging up so only the brow (highest view gain) crosses |
| 3 Materialisation | 14-26 | The hands resolve fully (dissolve to 0, the silver edge burning off). The face flips A to B, striped, the curtain sweeping with the camera. The key light rises on the muzzle. **The eyes last:** the lids open (rotation), the iris program brightens, and the catchlight light comes up at 24-26 s | `timeline.hands`, `timeline.face` (bias), `timeline.eyes` (lid rotation, `catch` intensity) |
| 4 Presence | 26-44 | Hold. Slow gaze drift (noise), 2 blinks (keyed at about 31 s and 39 s), a finger tightening (keyed at 35 s, plus audio). One restrained sweep across the face (36-40 s) exposes the ridges. The camera's lateral drift does the lenticular work | Timeline keys plus the audio routes (§4) |
| 5 Disturbance | 44-50 | The gaze locks on the camera; the head slips 2 degrees and 0.04 out of alignment with the bars; 3 stepped view jumps with crosstalk; the face half-flips to the silver negative and back, like another depth layer showing through; a highlight crosses the eyes | `timeline.disturb`: head node offset, the camera step track (add mode), the echo on, the second flip bias |
| 6 Return to ether | 50-60 | **Different rates:** the eyes go first (the lids half-close, the catchlight off by 52 s, but the cornea glint lingers as a trace until 56 s). The muzzle flips back to A (the curtain the other way). The hands dissolve (silver edges, 52-58 s). The last trace is one bar glint at 59 s | `timeline.recede` (and the inverse ramps of the others) |
| 7 Reset | 60-64 | All values at Phase-1 levels. The camera mid-sweep (continuous) | Keys equal at 64 = 0 |

**Live control:**
- a pad forces the cycle to restart: a `timeline` source reset (the existing per-source reset);
- a CC scrubs the "presence" depth (all reveal amounts times `control.presence`, rest 1.0).
- States are not needed for the slice.

## 4. Audio mappings (current signals only)

- The mix kick, snare and hat are **retired** on main (764dd528, daf93949). Low attacks come from `response.low`, any
  attack from `response.onset`, and drum identities only from a drums-role source (`role.drums.kick/snare/hat`).
- Every route takes `depthSource: visual.presence`. That is an interpret product of the cycle's presence envelope (0
  in Ether, 1 in Presence) and `control.presence`. Audio decorates the apparition; it never summons it.
- Every event route has an envelope (decay ≥ 150 ms) and a clamp. Nothing flashes in dense passages. Quiet passages
  still play the whole cycle.

## 5. Cost

**Probe measurements: not yet taken.**
- The headless bench (`--range 1.0: --frames 360 --bench-json`, 1920x1080, P1-P3 x2) waited more than 45 minutes on

## 6. Engine changes (ADR 1690-1699)

**None is required for stop 1.** Every mechanism above is an existing op, effect, track or source. The probe proved
the three new ones (ridges, flip, crosstalk) on existing ops. Reserved, each only if the build shows the need, each
with a test that the feature switched off is byte-identical:

| ADR | Candidate | Trigger | Test |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1690 | A `sin` (or `cos`) op in material programs | Only if `palette` as a cosine proves awkward for the ridge and stripe terms (it works today, 2 ops) | CPU/GPU parity, plus a program without the op hashes the same |
| 1691 | Program `normal` frame from a named axis (`frame: "world-x"`) | Only if the derivative cotangent frame twists the ridge direction on the scan's curvature (watch the brow and cheek in the stop-1 clip) | Pixel diff with the frame option off = golden |
| 1692 | Per-node phase offset on a `timeline` source (one cycle, many staggered parts) | Only if the 7+ timeline sources become unwieldy | `[timeline]` unit test, plus a source without an offset is byte-identical |
| 1693-1699 | Reserved for the art pass | | |
