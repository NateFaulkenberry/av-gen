# Canyon Phase 1c: corridor warmth, the opening, near-rock crispness

This follows the coordinator's Phase 1c brief, after the owner's review of Phase 1b. Media is in
`~/Desktop/av-gen-review/101-strange-attractor/canyon-p1c/`:
- `00` is the 46 s flythrough at 1080p30, 2x supersampled;
- `01-06` are the checkpoint pairs against gate2e;
- `07` shows the former flicker spikes as four consecutive frames each, Phase 1 against 1c;
- `08` has the flicker numbers.

## 1. River corridor warmth (18.5 s, 22.5 s)

This is solved as art direction: **the sun is raised from 9° to 14°** (azimuth unchanged).
- I compared six sun placements at both checkpoints: elevation 9, 12 and 15° at azimuth 70, plus 9° at 55 and 85, and
  12° at 60.
- At 12° the right-hand slab at 18.5 s comes into sun and throws the gold reflection down the river. At 14–15° the
  22.5 s slab and sandbank do too.
- Moving the azimuth alone never lit them.
- The cloud key direction follows the sun.

The reach fix (ADR-1858) is untouched, so the shadows are still stable: flicker max is **0.16** on the final clip
(Phase 1b 0.25, Phase 1 6.23, gate2e 0.14).

## 2. The opening (1 s)

**The vertical streaks: the band coordinate, not the geometry (ADR-1860).** I traced them by elimination:

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Shadows off | Streaks still there |
| No material program | Fainter streaks still there |
| Vertex normals rendered as colour | Smooth |
| Baked occlusion rendered as colour | Smooth |
| Max triangle edge capped at 8 m, then 4 m (+1.5%, +5% triangles) | Streaks unchanged; the triangle count barely moved |
| Band coordinate rendered as a colour cycle | **Sawtooth band lines down the near face** |

uv.x carried the *warped* band coordinate. The warp (two sines) bends within a triangle that drops several bands down
a cliff, so linear interpolation zigzags every band line. The colour, the bedding planes and the ledge normals all
followed it.

Now uv.x carries the coordinate before the warp, and the program warps it per pixel. The unit test measures the mean
band error at the centroids of tall triangles: 0.035 bands now, against 0.170 for the warped attribute.

**The pale "snow" patch on the near rock** was a flat top in sun, painted in a pale mauve-and-band mix with no surface
texture. Three changes fix it:
- Flats are now warm sand that takes 25% of the band colour.
- They get stronger albedo breakup.
- The low-frequency relief normal now applies to flats as well as walls.

It reads as sunlit sandstone (`01-*`).

## 3. Near-rock crispness

The rock program is now generated by `examples/strange-attractor/build_rock_program.py` (63 of 64 ops).
- The ledges are stronger: tilt 1.5, where 1b had 1.2.
- The laminations are stronger: 0.22, where 1b had 0.18.
- The bedding plane is darker and tighter.
- The cream band stop is warmer and darker. It was washing out to near-white in sun.

**Across-strata breakup:** each ledge's strength varies along the wall with the low-frequency relief noise (0.35 to
1.45x), so ledges come and go instead of running as perfect stripes.

A true across-strata tilt isn't possible in this frame. The tangent frame's bitangent comes from uv.y, which is near
constant. It is left for Phase 2's ops budget.

Shimmer is controlled: the laminations and relief fade out between 280 and 700 m. The flicker metric fell from 1b to
1c (p90 0.050 → 0.037).

## 4. Lower priority

**The 18.5 s "framing difference" is not a camera difference.**
- Three repeated single-frame renders of one scene are bit-identical.
- The two SSR variants I had compared differ only below the waterline: the top half's mean difference is 0.00, the
  bottom half's 2.30.
- What looked like a shifted wall was its reflection changing.

The flight camera is deterministic, and single-frame renders match the clip.

**Cloud cost (18 ms) is not reduced in 1c.**
- The engine's lobe clouds march from the first lobe entry to the last exit. With thin streak lobes at grazing angles
  that is kilometres of empty steps, and every step evaluates every lobe hit.
- At 24 steps they cost 5.4 ms but dissolve into dither speckle.
- The right fix is an analytic cirrus term in the procedural sky. That would be engine work with its own ADR, and the
  owner should decide on it.
