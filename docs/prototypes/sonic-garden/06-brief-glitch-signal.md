# Prototype GLITCH SIGNAL

Create a glitchy, cybernetic audiovisual environment centered around **digital signal corruption, data streams, scanlines, fragmentation, and unstable rendering**.

This should NOT be a realistic cyberpunk city.

Do not build:

* skyscrapers
* streets
* cars
* neon city blocks
* realistic computers
* cyberpunk buildings
* generic Tron environments

Instead, create a **stylized digital space that looks like a beautiful piece of corrupted media or an unstable graphics system.**

The environment should still have strong spatial composition and depth.

---

## Core Visual Concept

Imagine flying through a gigantic digital signal that has partially broken apart.

The world could consist of:

* large architectural slabs
* grids
* scanline surfaces
* wireframe terrain
* fragmented panels
* data ribbons
* rectangular image fragments
* geometric fields
* glowing vector paths
* distorted silhouettes
* layered planes
* pixel-like particles
* chromatic offsets
* digital noise

These elements should form an actual **environmental composition** rather than a collection of objects floating randomly.

The viewer should feel like they are moving through a corrupted digital world.

---

# Camera

The camera should continuously travel through the environment.

Possible composition:

**foreground**

Large fragments / scanline structures / glitching surfaces close to the camera.

**midground**

A coherent digital pathway or tunnel through the environment.

**background**

Large-scale structures receding into darkness, haze, or a bright digital horizon.

Create strong perspective and a sense of forward motion.

The viewer should always understand approximately where they are going.

---

# Visual Language

Explore:

* RGB channel separation
* chromatic aberration
* scanlines
* pixel displacement
* image tearing
* horizontal data corruption
* vertical data streams
* block corruption
* geometric fragmentation
* flickering surfaces
* digital trails
* wireframe overlays
* CRT-inspired artifacts
* datamoshing-like transitions
* noise fields
* pixel sorting-inspired movement
* geometric slicing
* screen-space distortion
* color quantization
* posterization
* rapid palette shifts

Use these as **art direction**, not random filters.

The goal is:

> beautiful digital corruption

rather than:

> turn every post-processing effect on simultaneously.

---

# Color

Use an aggressive digital palette.

Possible combinations:

* electric cyan
* saturated magenta
* violet
* acid green
* deep blue
* white-hot highlights
* occasional orange/red corruption

Use strong color separation.

Some moments can be almost monochromatic before suddenly breaking into multiple RGB channels.

Color should itself be highly reactive.

---

# Geometry

Keep geometry relatively simple.

Use:

* planes
* boxes
* strips
* grids
* tubes
* ribbons
* low-poly forms
* simple extrusions
* particles

The complexity should come from **arrangement, deformation, layering and rendering**, not expensive meshes.

A simple rectangular structure can become visually sophisticated when:

* sections shift independently
* pieces tear apart
* textures/patterns slide across it
* RGB channels separate
* geometry pulses
* sections temporarily disappear
* fragments trail behind it

---

# Glitch Behavior

Glitches should occur at multiple scales.

### Micro glitches

Constant subtle:

* scanline movement
* pixel noise
* color separation
* small flickers
* tiny geometry displacement

### Meso glitches

Triggered periodically:

* surfaces tearing
* geometry shifting
* horizontal displacement
* fragments popping in/out
* data streams changing direction
* temporary color inversion

### Macro glitches

Triggered by strong musical events:

* entire environmental sections fragment
* camera briefly passes through a corrupted transition
* large structures split into layers
* RGB channels separate dramatically
* environment rapidly reassembles
* giant waves of digital corruption travel through the world

The result should feel **alive and unstable**, not continuously noisy.

Contrast is important.

---

# Audio Modulation

This prototype should demonstrate extremely deep modulation.

### Bass

Control:

* large-scale geometry displacement
* environmental pulse
* distortion strength
* major RGB separation
* scale of structures
* large data waves

### Midrange

Control:

* geometry slicing
* movement of digital panels
* grid deformation
* color transitions
* data-stream density

### High frequencies

Control:

* scanline intensity
* particle activity
* fine noise
* sparks
* edge highlights
* small displacement

### Onsets

Trigger:

* glitch bursts
* horizontal tearing
* geometry fragmentation
* color inversion
* sudden displacement
* data explosions

### Spectral brightness

Control:

* overall visual sharpness
* palette brightness
* distortion character
* atmospheric density

### Tempo

Control:

* repeating scan patterns
* data-flow speed
* environmental oscillation
* rhythmic geometry movement

### MIDI

Notes can:

* spawn digital fragments
* illuminate structures
* create data streams
* trigger localized glitches
* change palette regions

Velocity can control glitch intensity.

Pitch can control vertical position, hue, or spatial depth.

Sustained notes can maintain persistent digital structures.

---

# Critical Artistic Constraint

Do NOT let the glitch effects destroy the composition.

There must always be a recognizable underlying environment.

Think:

> beautiful environment → corrupted by signal

not:

> random glitch effects → hopefully becomes an environment.

The viewer should be able to see the underlying structure even when it is being heavily corrupted.

---

# Signature Visual Moment

Build at least one major sequence where a strong musical event causes the entire environment to undergo a controlled **digital collapse and reconstruction**.

For example:

1. Stable geometric/data environment.
2. Subtle RGB separation begins.
3. Horizontal sections begin drifting.
4. Surfaces fragment into rectangular pieces.
5. The environment briefly becomes almost entirely corrupted.
6. A large wave of color/data passes through the camera.
7. The world reconstructs itself into a new configuration.
8. Audio reactivity continues from the new state.

This should feel like a deliberate audiovisual event, not a random effect.

---

# Overall Target

The visual reference is:

**experimental digital art + music video + corrupted graphics system + high-end audiovisual performance**

NOT:

**cyberpunk city + neon + rain.**

It should be colorful, aggressive, strange, highly animated and extremely responsive to audio while retaining a strong underlying spatial composition.

The key idea is:

> **A beautiful digital world that is constantly threatening to break apart.**

Keep the underlying geometry simple.

Make the visual system sophisticated.

Make the glitch behavior musical.

Make the composition intentional.

Make the result look like something that could only exist as an audiovisual artwork.
