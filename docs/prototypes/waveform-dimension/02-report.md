## The scene

**A vast sculpture of nested curved sheets: the level sets of one standing interference field, which the music
bends, parts, folds and lights.**

- **The form (static, its identity).** The CARRIER is the standing interference of three long spherical waves
  (wavelengths 128-172 m) from points far outside the frame. One wave leads, so its shells give four nested sheets
  that read as offset contours of one field. The two weaker waves bend those sheets into saddles and press
  neighbours together or apart, so the spacing is uneven.
  - A second, shorter interference, the APERTURE, cuts a few large, smooth, off-centre openings.
  - The sheets are extracted at build time by surface nets (`tools/waveform_dimension/levelsets.py`) and cut exactly
    at the aperture's zero (`clip_mesh`). They stay opaque, never cut by a masked discard.
  - The camera is an elevated three-quarter arc (radius 140 m, 92 m up, a 70 degree sweep over 160 s, 24 mm), looking
    down into the nest. It is never inside one opening looking down it (no portal or tunnel), and the sculpture
    fills the frame.
- **The music (live).** One ADR-1400 resonator with four families, a source each:
  - **Bass:** point modes, 70-44 m. They bend broad lobes (5 m x (1 + 1.3 bass)) and part the nested sheets: each
    sheet moves along its normal in proportion to its place in the nest, keeping order. The song's build holds them
    further apart.
  - **Low mids:** one plane-wave family heard by THREE readers in frames 66-69 degrees apart. The readers share the
    modes, so the sum is coherent: a curved relative of the triply periodic minimal surfaces, folding the sheets in
    every direction, never a grid. Held sound grows them (standing patterns accumulate, with a longer memory). The
    sound's brightness moves their focus (modal exchange between wavelengths).
  - **Mids:** plane waves, 24-15 m. Travelling bands whose drift widens with spectral change.
  - **Highs:** fine ridges, via CC 6.
  - **Kicks:** each low onset launches a spherical shell that lifts the sheets it passes (7 m) and lights them crimson.
  - **Silence:** a `drive` floor keeps every family faintly ringing, so silence is restrained, never frozen.
- **The light.**
  - A grazing key, a rim from behind and colour on the lit faces.
  - The glow is a contour band of the families' reinforcement (|D| 3-6 m), so it rings the lobes and runs along the
    folds. The palette's accent appears only in its cores (|D| > 9 m), plus the kick's travelling band.
  - Every sheet's cut edge carries a dim contour line, and the outer sheets' a crimson one, brightened by hats and
    snares.
- **Colour.** Eight designed palettes (ADR-1043 states bound by role), changed by the ADR-1420 selector:
  - It advances on the phrase (`beat.phrasePulse`), with a 24 s hold and a 5 s OKLab blend, in a composed order.
  - With live input and no tempo, it advances on a large sustained change in the music (ADR-1421), or after 75 s of
    sameness.
  - The palettes are Indigo Crimson (the owner's, first), Ultramarine Gold, Ember Rose, Teal Amber, Magenta Lime,
    Glacier Violet, Jade Coral and Cyan Fuchsia.

The GPU breakdown at High (Feline), by pass:
- scene pass: 22.0 ms;
- depth prepass: 4.8 ms;
- the volume bake (`sim`): 2.6 ms;
- post: 0.2 ms.

The scene pass is lit PBR plus the 25-op program, over about 80k vertices whose deformer chain is evaluated four times
(the finite-difference normal). Below Medium, resolution stops helping much: the vertex stage (deformers in both
passes) is the floor.

How it got there, in order:
- the representation (C, 121 ms);
- geometric cuts instead of a masked discard;
- the volume bake of the slow families (ADR-1440);
- a 14-op program (25 after the palettes);
- the depth prepass;
- the mids family evaluated live (it striated when baked);
- 1.8 m cells.

Measured and rejected:
- live opening size by a masked edge band: 10-12 ms in the prepass;
- slabs with lit rims: double the vertices, and faces that part with the music.

## Known limitations

- **The openings keep their designed shapes.** A live opening size (masked band) cost 10-12 ms and is off. The gaps
  between the nested sheets breathe instead, so "openings enclosing or subdividing" is only partly delivered.
- **The form is static at the largest scale.** The carrier's level sets are fixed meshes, so the music moves, bends
  and parts the sheets but never changes their topology.
- **The glow's contour bands** can read as a marbled pattern in dense passages on bright palettes.
- **The standing-wave state is the subtlest.** Sustained tones mostly grow folds and lengthen the memory; there is no
  dedicated light for "a standing pattern has formed".
- **Ultra misses 30 fps** (34.7 ms GPU). Auto settles on High.
- **The sheets' outer bound** is an ellipsoid. At some arc positions its rim shows as a soft silhouette at the top of
  the frame.
- **The palette advance on `beat.phrasePulse`** needs a tempo. Live input relies on ADR-1421's change trigger, whose
  thresholds are Cymatic World's defaults, untuned for this scene.
- **MIDI routes** are verified by load (32 routes and 15 palette bindings, 0 warnings, no unknown targets logged; the render warns that `control.*` routes are
  live-only, as expected), not with hardware.

## Next highest-value improvements

1. **Openings that change shape live without a masked discard.** For example, a vertex-stage collapse of the edge
   band (a deformer that pulls edge-band vertices onto the rest edge by a routed amount), or a second, coarser set of
   pre-extracted cuts crossfaded on the phrase.
2. **A "standing pattern" light:** the glow band widening and steadying with the memory envelope (the slow figure)
   during sustained tones.
3. **Cheaper vertices.** A combined single deformer (the front and the spacing folded into the bake) would cut the
   four-times chain evaluation. That could give Ultra.
4. **Tune ADR-1421's change thresholds** on live input for this scene's signals.

## Quality pass (2026-10-17, `art/resonator-quality`)

The owner's quality brief and colour-field addendum: `docs/prototypes/resonator-quality/` (report `01-report.md`).

- **Colour.** Travelling chromatic waves replace the one colour per sheet. A cyclic OKLab ramp (ADR-1470) runs
  through four analogous per-palette roles, along a world axis, bent by D, a step per nested sheet, with the phase
  integrated from the music.
- **Light.** A broad light from the camera's side (`front`, 1.2), and the fill raised from 0.35 to 1.2. Vignette
  0.4 → 0.2.
- **Glow.** A smooth rise with |D| replaces the 3-6 m contour band, whose flat-edged rings read as banding. 30 ops.
- **Geometry** (`levelsets.py`). The cut's crossings are found by bisection on the cut and put back on the sheet,
  the opening/bound corner is a smooth minimum, and fragments under 400 m^2 are dropped. That removes the shards,
  notches and horns at the edges.
- **Build switch:** `WD_DEBUG=albedo|normals` (review views).
