**What it is.** One immense sheet of satin, about 360 m of cloth. It enters as a broad wall of pleats from the left
foreground, and every pleat converges on an invisible ring right of centre: the *gather*, the focal seam. Beyond the
ring the same cloth opens into a tall sail that billows and turns over into the dark. It is one object, a single
continuous mesh. The music excites it as a resonator:
- bass bows it and sends a swell out along the wall;
- the middle band creases it;
- kicks launch fronts from the gather and snares launch fronts across the wall;
- treble runs a fine sheen along the folds' turning edges.

Eight palettes turn the same sculpture into eight materials. The project's palette selector (ADR-1420, built here
and shared with Cymatic World) chooses them, locks them, and moves through them on phrase boundaries.
