# Talking Heads, fx1: four secondary natures, and one to four busts

Media: `~/Desktop/av-gen-review/92-talking-heads/fx1/`, made by `tools/talking_heads/fx1.sh <work dir>`. The brief is
`14-brief-secondary-fx.md` and the plan `15-fx-plan.md`. The mouth, eyes, lids, neck and bust animation and the
background are unchanged, as the owner asked.

## The drop shadow

It was the stop-6 ghost copies of the head, seated 3 cm behind it and offset sideways. They are gone. The only copies
left belong to the synth's optical profile:
- rim-only: the opacity is the fresnel itself (power 4), so a copy is invisible face-on;
- their own colour as the body, never black;
- split apart for about 120 ms on the synth's onsets, and drifting slightly apart on a held note.

## Why bass and synth looked alike, and what each is now

The audit is in `15-fx-plan.md`. Every head had the same five effects at different weights. Now each source is a
different phenomenon, recognisable with the colours neutral and the rays dark (`profiles-neutral-no-rays/`,
`01-profiles-side-by-side-neutral-no-rays.mp4`).

**FRAGMENT (drums, kept).** Transient-only: the bust shakes, slices sideways (a world-space shear) and stripes with
hashed signal bands on each opening, and recovers in about 120 ms. Nothing happens between hits.

**LIQUID (bass).** Continuous and heavy.
- A long wave travels up the bust: about one wavelength over its height, sideways and toward the camera. Its height
  follows the bass's smoothed level and its travel is integrated from it.
- A strong low onset swells it, and it settles over 1.2-1.5 s.
- Each onset sends a thin, irregular pressure ring out from the neck; a strong one sends a second from the chest. The
  rings keep travelling after the note and fade before they are wide (the light is the envelope to the power 2.5).
  They are unlit and take ADR-1673's fast path.

**OPTICAL (synth).** Phrase-scale and optical; the silhouette never moves.
- A scan band sweeps down the bust on a saw LFO whose rate rises with the synth's level. It draws a bright line and
  a wide trail where the porcelain darkens toward black and back: surface inversion.
- Veins of light (the cell edges of a drifting Worley pattern) grow over a held note.
- The rim-only copies split apart on each onset.

**GRAIN (a vocal or other source, and the hero on a plain mix).**
- Dark patches dissolve into the surface and drift down as the level rises, faintly lit inside.
- The head sheds grains that drift up and away, in bursts on its onsets: up to 500 GPU particles per grain bust.

**Signal types differ by design.** Drums use their gate's rises. Bass uses its smoothed low band and onset envelopes,
liquid in time. Synth uses its sustained mid band, an LFO and onset snaps. Grain uses level and onset bursts. The busts
therefore never move together.

## One to four busts

- **Slots per role,** on the existing source abstraction (ADR-1630): drums, bass, synth, and vocal, else other. A
  slot is shown while its role is active, whether the source is a stem or a live input. So the count follows the
  assigned sources, and a second source of the same role adds no fifth bust.
- **Layouts per count** (`layouts/`): a hero; an asymmetric pair; the stop-5 stagger; two forward and two back,
  turned in, at 0.92-0.95 scale, none cut by the frame. A bust glides to its new place in 0.7 s. An inactive one
  shrinks away in 0.5 s and grows back in when its source returns.
- **No sources:** one hero on the mix, the grain profile on its rate-limited mix path.
- **Arriving and leaving:** `06-sources-arrive-and-leave.mp4`. Drums play throughout, bass arrives at 70 s, synth
  plays 80-100 s, and the mix as `other` plays 90-105 s, so the count runs 1, 2, 3, 4, 3, 2.

**Checks** (`checks/`):
- `lifecycle.txt` checks every window: the count right, shown busts full-size, hidden busts shrunk with their gate
  shut and rays dark, no shared places. It passes.
- `isolation.txt`: with four sources, muting any one leaves the other three bit-identical on every frame. It passes.
- `rays.txt`: every ray tracks its mouth.
- `seam.txt`: every profile's surface effects at full strength, on all four slots. The result is in the coordinator
  report.

## Not done

- **Runtime profile choice.** A slot's profile is chosen in the scene's slot table (`HEADS[...]["profile"]`), not
  yet by a MIDI control. The fourth slot's default is grain.
- **More sources than roles.** Two sources of the same role share one bust. The busts are per role, by the existing
  `role.<role>` signals, which publish the first active source of each role.
