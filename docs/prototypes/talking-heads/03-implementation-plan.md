# Talking Heads: implementation plan

Prep for an implementation agent. It rests on `00-brief.md` (the owner's spec, verbatim), `01-research-assets.md`
and `02-research-engine.md`. Live target: 1080p30. Category: Sonic Abstract. ADR range: 1670-1689. House rules apply:
no compat shims, no helper agents, the 2-agent cap, the owner approves visuals before any merge, and GPU work goes
through `tools/gpu-lock.sh`.

## 1. The smallest complete vertical slice

One scene, `talking_heads`, generated by `tools/sonic_vfx/scenes/talking_heads.py` into
`examples/sonic-abstract/talking-heads.{json,scene.json}`. **No engine change.** It contains:

- **Bust.** A procedural mesh node `th-bust` (`meshes/th-bust.glb`, about 30 k triangles, from
  `tools/talking_heads/make_bust.py`), the `th-porcelain` program on the shell, and `th-mouth` on the mouth interior.
- **Emitters.** One hero mouth cone, 6 mouth rays and 2 rays per eye. Each is a `lightBeam` owned by an authored spot
  light riding `th-bust`, placed from the anchors file. They come from one reusable kit helper, `emitter_fan(...)`
  (task 3).
- **Effect A (vibration).** A `shake` effect on `th-bust`, plus two gltf ghost children (`th-bust-ghost.glb`, BLEND,
  program `th-ghost`) whose offset and opacity are routed.
- **Effect B (chromatic possession).** The wash inside `th-porcelain`, plus `glow` and a travelling `pulse` on the
  bust.
- **Effect C (tearing).** T1, T2 and T3:
  - T1, full-screen `post/glitch/tear` and `post/split`, transient-gated, reading as local on the near-black ether;
  - T2, material bands (`quantize`) in `th-porcelain`;
  - T3, a Sine shear deformer on `th-bust`.
- **Life.** A `float` effect, two `noise` sources driving rotation, and an onset nod through a spring.
- **World.**
  - Ether: sky gradient from indigo to violet, faint haze, about 40 slow motes.
  - Lights: an unshadowed warm key and a violet rim.
  - Camera: `drift_camera`, 50 mm lens.
- **Signals.** `signal.awake`, `signal.voice`, `signal.snare`, `signal.hat` and `signal.overload`, plus
  drum_signals.py's `signal.kick`, `signal.kickEnv` and `signal.bassLevel`.
- **Control.** Eight control channels (rest 0.5, pickup) and four pads. States (Dormant, Communicating, Overload) are
  pad-forced only.
- **Index.** An `INDEX_ENTRY` that names the MIDI map. Register it only after the owner approves the visuals.

## 3. Binding table (initial defaults: tune by ear on three sources, never one)

Conventions:
- **Depth.** Every row except life rows L1-L2 takes `depthSource: visual.<feature>Gate`. That is an interpret
  product, same frame, of `control.master`, `control.<feature>` and `signal.awake`, with gain 2: at rest
  (0.5 x 0.5 x 1) it reads 0.5, so the amounts below are already doubled.
- **Envelopes** are `attackMs` / `decayMs` on the route chain. Events go through `attackMs` 0.
- **Amounts** are clamped in the chain (`clampEnabled`) to the stated maximum.

## 5. Budget (GPU 1080p, live)

Measured probe: 4.0-4.7 ms p50 for the full stack (02 §4). The budget leaves room for programs, particles and blended
ghosts:

| Item | ms |
|---|---|
| Bust with programs | ≤ 1.5 |
| Beams (hero + 6 rays + 4 eye rays) | ≤ 2.0 |
| Ghosts (blended) | ≤ 0.5 |
| Tear, split, echo | ≤ 0.4 |
| Bloom and the rest of post | ≤ 1.0 |
| Sky, haze, motes | ≤ 1.0 |
| **Total** | **p50 ≤ 7, p95 ≤ 10** (limit 29.3) |

CPU: ≤ 8 ms per frame. Lights: ≤ 14 riding spots plus 2. Shells: ≤ 16 of 128. If over budget, find the dominant pass
in `--bench-json` `gpuPassMedianMs`. It will be the hero cone's screen coverage: shorten its length or lower its
`steps` (10 to 6) before touching the bust.

## 7. Risks

1. **Procedural bust parenting gltf ghosts is untested** (probe: gltf to gltf). Check in task 2. Fallback: a gltf bust,
   with tearing reduced to T1 + T2.
2. **The tear catches haze or motes.** Keep haze low, or take G2.
3. **The hero cone fills the frame when the composition is tight.** Coverage costs and washes out. Cap length and
   angle; frame with room.
4. **The beams look like dusty searchlights at defaults** (the probe did). The thin-ray settings are in 02 §2.1.
5. **Mixes without vocals.** `signal.voice` falls back to `audio.mid`, which is busy on dense mixes. Clamp M2/M3 and
   rely on S1's gate.
6. **Overload saturates.** S5 is self-limiting through the 12 s follower plus peak-hold release. Check on Trench's
   loudest section.
7. **The `+Z` facing.** Keep explicit local aims (02 §6.5).
8. **Light-owned beams die at light intensity 0.** Never route `lights/<id>/intensity`; route `fx/<id>/intensity`.
9. **The CPU suite counts as GPU work.** Serialise suites under the lock (docs/testing.md).
