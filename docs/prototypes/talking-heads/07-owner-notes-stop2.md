## Requested changes

**Soften the jaw.** No blunt nutcracker hinge. The opening should read as a natural, softer drop, with no dark wedges
at the sides of the chin and smoother edges where the jaw meets the face.

**Bust fixes**, from `11-adams-apple-and-neck-profile-32deg.png`, as the coordinator saw it:
- the chin piece separates, with a jagged, stepped edge and dark gaps under the chin and at the jaw line;
- the throat is lumpy and wavy, and the Adam's apple doesn't read as one clear bump;
- there is a notch at the back of the neck where it meets the body;
- the neck still reads long.

At every tilt the bust must stay one clean porcelain surface. Check close-ups at 0°, 20° and 32°, in profile and
three-quarter.

**Full mix.** Cap how often the gate fires on the mix fallback: a minimum interval and a stricter threshold, on the mix
path only. Stems and channels are the focus.

**Multi-head.**
- One head per source; the starter is the three Feline stems, Drums, Bass and Synth.
- Later, live input channels with roles replace the stems.
- For the drums head, pick kick only or kick plus snare, so the gate doesn't stay open.
- Space the heads well apart, with distinct headings, scan phases and beam colours.
- Stay inside the live budget: ghosts only on a head whose gate is open.
- Build the darting eyes for every head.
- Prefer one scene handling N heads from a source list.
