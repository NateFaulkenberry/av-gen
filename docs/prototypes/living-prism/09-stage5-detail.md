Stage 5 was kept modest: the frame is already full at Ultra, and the live headroom is thin (07). Only details that
fix a weakness or add life between musical events were added.

**Media:** `~/Desktop/av-gen-review/82-living-prism/stage5/`:

- `feline-typical-window.mp4`, with audio.

| **The spire's tip** | termination glow 0.6 to 0.4 | the upper third still whitened |
