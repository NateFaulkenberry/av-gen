# Rainbow World 07: Stage 3 report (the look-development scene, in AV Gen)

Stage 3 of the brief (§8). I built the look-development scene **in AV Gen** rather than in Blender, so every judgement
below is made on the engine's own frame: AgX, the procedural sky, GTAO, CSM.

The scene is generated by `examples/rainbow-world/lookdev/build_lookdev.py`, from the assets exported by
`examples/rainbow-world/blender/engine_assets.py`. The look itself (palette, material programs, environment, post)
lives in `examples/rainbow-world/blender/look.py` and is shared with the world builder. Five controlled views are
rendered through AV Gen at 1080p.

## 1. What the engine needed: four additive changes

Each change has one commit, a test and an ADR. Each is off by default and byte-identical when off.

| ADR | What it adds | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1545 | `castsShadow` on a procedural node | E5 measured 31.7 of 62.8 ms of grass in the shadow cascades. Grass, leaf cards, flowers and clouds now stay out. |
| 1540 | The fisheye lens: a remap of the rectilinear frame (`post/lens/fisheye`, `fisheyeModel`) | The clip's lens (01 §3, E1) |
| 1541 | `camera/splineUp`: a spline camera's up follows the spline's frame | The camera's gravity must turn with the land |
| 1542 | `rollTurns`: a closed spline's roll can wind whole turns | The knot twists one turn round the loop and must close without spinning back |

Tests: `[adr1545]`, `[adr1540]` (unit and GPU), `[adr1541]` and `[adr1542]`. All pass, along with the neighbouring
cases: the 45 spline cases, the camera roll and bank cases, and the 44 `[post]` GPU cases.

## 2. Iterations (v1 to v7) and what each fixed

| Version | Change | Read |
|---|---|---|
| v1 | First AV Gen assembly | The rainbow (an alpha-blended, front-faced tube with a spectral emissive texture) is **vivid under AgX**, which resolves U6 without a new render pass. The sky is pale, the greens lime, the grass lettuce-like, the clouds bubbly and lavender. The distant "echo" slabs read as debris. |
| v2-v3 | Smaller, denser grass; a deeper sky; the cloud sea placed lower | Better sky and grass; the cloud sea still reads as a plastic floor |
| v4 | The view target moved to 15 m | AV Gen's GTAO radius is 5% of the eye-to-target distance, which had made dark halos under every tuft. Echo slabs removed (a slab with an edge is a visible world edge; the knot's real strands fill those directions). Cloud meshes remeshed and smoothed (too smooth). |
| v5 | Clouds: billows kept, a soft silhouette (fresnel blend to the horizon colour), more warm/cool | The clouds read as painted volumes |
| v6 | **Colour measured against the references**: exposure +0.6 to +0.2, contrast 1.04 to 1.14, darker greens, a teal shade, a lower and warmer raking sun, flower drifts, small tree clusters | Value now matches R1-R4; contrast much closer |
| v7 | **The placement bug**: `band.surface_point` snapped to the nearest ring, so finite-difference normals pointed along the band. Every grass tuft leaned sideways and sat on a 1.4-6 m grid; in the world the camera was lifted forward, not up, and so rode inside the band. Now interpolated (`band.frame_lerp`). | Grass reads as continuous turf |

**Measured against the references** (mean HSV of each frame; greens and blues are the pixels in those hue bands with
saturation over 0.2):

|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | 0.45 | 0.64 | 0.41 / 0.48 | 0.52 / 0.65 |
| R3 | 0.57 | 0.58 | 0.60 / 0.24 | 0.68 / 0.53 |
| R4 | 0.44 | 0.56 | 0.48 / 0.43 | 0.47 / 0.58 |
| v1 close | 0.51 | 0.70 | 0.57 / 0.56 | 0.48 / 0.82 |

Saturation and green values are now in the references' range. The skies stay lighter than R3's deep teal (blue value
0.74-0.80 against 0.53-0.65). That is deliberate for now, because a darker sky makes the shaded faces of the knot
read black. It is the next lever for the looks (Stage 6 states).

## 3. The five weakest points at Stage 3 entry, and where they stand

| # | Weakness (06 §3) | Now |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Putty meadow, yellow grass | **Fixed:** turf colour shared by ground and grass (the same world-space OKLab ramp), micro-detail breakup, the normals bug |
| 2 | The cloud representation | **Decided: sculpted meshes** with an unlit painted program: wrapped N.L ramp, billow noise, warm underside, silver rim, silhouette dissolved to the horizon colour. About 2 ms against 77 ms for the lobe march. ADR-1543 is not needed. The clouds still read stylised, not R1-volumetric; this is Stage 6's main look item. |
| 3 | Rim lips | **Improved:** a crisper profile break (`band.py` profile v2), rocks as the cliff. A grass fringe and hanging roots remain for Stage 5. |
| 4 | Rainbow chroma and scale | **Fixed in AV Gen:** the blended tube with alpha replaces the sky behind it. It is vivid and spectral, and the secondary bow reads. |
| 5 | Cost of trees and grass | **Partly:** `castsShadow` off for grass, cards, flowers, clouds and rim rocks; rim rocks at ~300 triangles; grass at 24-50 triangles a tuft. The world is still over budget (§4). |

**Still open:**
- Flowers read as stalks (their heads are too small).
- The cloud-sea puffs read as flat plastic when close.
- The shade faces of the knot are dark (U3). They keep colour, but need light.

## 4. First numbers for the whole world (Stage 4 is under way)

The full knot is a 5.7 km loop, 1,914 rings, 24 chunks. It carries:
- 454k grass tufts in a corridor round the ride;
- 9k flowers;
- 73 trees in four families;
- 1,972 rim rocks;
- 5 rainbow arches;
- the cloud sea, banks and masses.

The headless live profile at Ultra, 1920x1080:

| Pass | GPU |
|---|---|
| **total** | **44.3 ms** |
| geometry | 30.8 ms |
| shadows | 12.5 ms |

The budget is 29.3 ms. Before rim rocks went to ~300 triangles with no shadows, the total was 54 ms. The geometry
pass is the target in Stage 8: grass density and reach, band LOD for far chunks, flower and tree LOD. The Auto ladder
would also hold 30 fps live by scaling resolution, but the aim is Ultra or High without it.
